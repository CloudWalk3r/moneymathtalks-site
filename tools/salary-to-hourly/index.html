Salary to Hourly Calculator

Convert an annual salary into hourly, weekly and monthly pay.

A yearly salary is hard to compare with hourly work or with another job that has different hours. Turning it into an hourly figure makes the comparison fair. Enter the yearly salary, the hours per week and the weeks worked per year. The calculator shows your hourly, weekly and monthly pay.

How it works

Hourly pay is the salary divided by hours per week times weeks per year. Weekly pay is the salary divided by weeks worked, and monthly pay is the salary divided by 12. The hours you enter matter a lot. If a job regularly needs extra unpaid hours, enter the hours you really work; the hourly figure will fall and show the true rate.

Worked example

A salary of 50,000.00 for 40 hours a week over 52 weeks works out to 24.04 an hour. That is 961.54 a week and 4,166.67 a month.

Notes

  • Enter the hours you actually work, not just the contract hours.
  • Use this to compare two offers with different hours on a fair basis.
  • Results are before tax and deductions.
  • Reduce the weeks if you take unpaid time off.

FAQ

Is this before or after tax?

Before tax. Take-home pay depends on local tax rules and deductions.

Why does monthly pay divide by 12?

A yearly salary is normally paid in 12 equal monthly parts, whatever the weeks.

How do I compare two job offers?

Run each offer with its real hours and compare the hourly figures.

Is this financial advice?

No. It is a conversion tool.
