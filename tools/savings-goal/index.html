Savings Goal Calculator

How much to save each month to reach a target, counting growth on what you already have.

A savings goal becomes much easier once it turns into one monthly number. This calculator works backwards from the target and the deadline to tell you that number. Enter the goal, the number of years, an expected yearly rate and anything you have already saved. The calculator shows the monthly amount needed, what your current savings grow to, and the total you would add.

How it works

First the calculator grows your current savings to the deadline. Whatever is left of the goal has to come from monthly saving. It then finds the monthly amount that, with interest added each month, reaches exactly that gap by the deadline. Because the deposits earn a little interest along the way, the total you add is slightly less than the gap itself. The longer the deadline, the more months share the work, so the monthly amount drops quickly as you add time.

Worked example

Say the goal is 10,000.00 in 3 years at 4.00%, and you already have 1,000.00 saved. Those savings grow to 1,127.27 by the deadline. To cover the rest you need to save 232.38 a month, which adds up to 8,365.77 of your own money. Interest makes up the small difference.

Notes

  • Try a longer deadline before deciding a goal is out of reach.
  • Set the rate to 0 to see the plain amount you need without any growth.
  • Automating the monthly amount on payday makes it easier to stick with.
  • Rerun the numbers whenever your current savings change.

FAQ

What if I have nothing saved yet?

Enter 0 for current savings. The monthly amount will rise to cover the whole goal.

Why is the total I add less than the goal?

Your existing savings and the interest on each deposit cover part of the goal.

What rate should I enter?

Whatever rate your savings actually earn. If you are unsure, compare a low and a higher figure.

Is this financial advice?

No. It is a planning tool that shows the maths for the numbers you choose.
